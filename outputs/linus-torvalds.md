# Linux Foundation — Discovery Brief
**Linus Torvalds, Fellow / Linux kernel lead maintainer** · Prepared 2026-06-30

## 1. Snapshot (≤3 lines)
- A non-profit that provides a neutral home for developers to collaborate on open-source software, hosting projects across cloud, networking, blockchain, AI, and hardware.
- Industry: open-source / technology non-profit · Size: ~956 employees, 3,000+ member orgs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux_Foundation), [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/the-linux-foundation/5a1d9730230000520085c0d1/employee-directory)) · HQ: San Francisco, CA ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux_Foundation)).

## 2. Why now — the trigger
- The Foundation is in an aggressive open-source AI build-out in 2026: it formed the Agentic AI Foundation (170+ members in under four months), launched the OpenSharing and DNS-AID AI projects, and published a 2026 State of Tech Talent report flagging an AI skills imperative ([Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation), [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/blog/ai-open-source-and-the-skills-imperative-unpacking-the-2026-state-of-tech-talent-europe-report)).
- CAVEAT: Linus Torvalds is a globally recognized technologist, not a non-technical learner. He is not a genuine buyer for entry-level "AI training for non-technical teams." This brief researches the org; contact-specific buying signals are Unknown.

- The realistic fit is the Foundation's own non-technical staff (events, marketing, operations) rather than its engineers — our AI-adoption and repetitive-task-automation training maps to that population. Tied to the org's own 2026 skills-gap messaging.
- Proof point: we helped a client draw their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — relevant to an org actively expanding AI program headcount across many new foundations/projects.

## 5. Discovery questions
1. As the Foundation spins up the Agentic AI Foundation and projects like OpenSharing, how are your non-technical teams (events, marketing, ops) expected to use AI day-to-day?
2. Your 2026 State of Tech Talent report calls out an AI skills gap — does that gap show up internally among LF's own business staff, or only in the broader ecosystem you survey?
3. With 170+ member orgs joining AAIF so quickly, has the pace created repetitive coordination/admin work that AI could automate for your staff?
4. Do you have a standard internal approach to AI governance, security, and best practices for employees using AI tools?
5. Who owns upskilling for the non-engineering side of the Foundation, and what does success look like for them in the next two quarters?

## 6. Opener
"With the Agentic AI Foundation scaling past 170 members and your own 2026 talent report flagging an AI skills gap, I wanted to ask how the Foundation is bringing its non-technical teams up to speed — that's exactly the gap we close."
