## 4. Value hypothesis
- Figma is shipping AI faster than most non-technical teams can absorb it (agent, Skills, connectors, credit-metered usage). Our AI Training for non-technical teams maps directly to AI adoption, best practices, and AI governance/security — so design, marketing, and sales staff use these tools confidently and within policy rather than burning metered AI credits on trial-and-error.
- Most relevant proof point: we helped a client draw their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — directly useful as Figma's AI features reshape who does what across design and adjacent teams.
