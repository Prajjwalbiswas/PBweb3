- Builds an AI customer-support agent that autonomously resolves e-commerce support tickets (order/delivery "where is my order" queries, returns, refunds, cancellations) inside a team's existing helpdesk.
- Industry: AI / e-commerce customer-support software (SaaS) · Size: early-stage startup, likely <20 (Unknown exact) · HQ: London, UK.

## 2. Why now — the trigger
- Very early-stage company: SHIPHERD LTD is a recently registered UK company (Companies House no. 16003689) and the product is live/in production — the window where teams are being built and AI ways-of-working are set from scratch, before bad habits form.
- The product itself is agentic AI; the founders are shipping AI to customers but the surrounding non-technical functions (sales, marketing, ops/support design) likely have no formal AI-adoption playbook or governance yet — exactly when a training partner is cheapest to onboard.

## 4. Value hypothesis
- A founder building an AI product still has a non-technical go-to-market and ops layer (sales, marketing, support design) that needs to use AI well and safely; we train those teams on AI adoption, automating repetitive work, and governance/security best practices — letting a small team punch above its headcount.
- Proof point: we helped a client draw their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — directly relevant to a sub-20-person startup deciding which roles to hire vs. automate as it scales.

## 5. Discovery questions
1. As a founder shipping agentic AI, how are your non-technical functions — sales, marketing, support design — actually using AI day-to-day right now?
2. Where does repetitive, manual work still eat your small team's time outside the product itself?
3. Do you have any internal rules yet for how staff use AI tools — what's allowed, what data can go in, who reviews outputs?
4. As you plan your next hires, how are you deciding which roles to fill with people versus automate?
5. What would "good" look like for AI fluency across your team six months from now?

## 6. Opener
"Monish — you're shipping agentic AI to your e-commerce customers, but I'm curious whether your own non-technical team has a playbook for using AI as fast and as safely as the product does. That gap is what we close."

## 7. Watch-outs (include ONLY if real)
- Founder is deeply AI-native (ex-Amazon, building an AI product) and may feel his team needs no outside AI training — position the offering at the non-technical/governance layer, not technical upskilling.
- Tiny, likely pre-revenue/seed-stage company: budget and headcount are constrained; lead with ROI (50% faster role decisions, automating repetitive work) over breadth of curriculum.
- Competitors in this space (BrainStation, General Assembly, AI Academy) may also be prospecting founder-led startups.
