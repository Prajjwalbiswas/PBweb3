## 4. Value hypothesis
- A boutique consultancy runs on repetitive, high-touch knowledge work — school research, application drafting, family correspondence across time zones. AI training for a non-technical team could automate the repetitive prep and standardise quality, freeing the founders for high-value placement and relationship work.
- They handle sensitive minor/family data across the UK, Nigeria and Saudi Arabia, so AI governance, security and best-practice training is a credible second hook. Proof point: we helped a client map their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — relevant to a small team deciding who should own which AI-augmented tasks.

## 5. Discovery questions
1. Walk me through where your consultants spend the most repetitive hours today — school research, application drafting, or family follow-ups across your London/Abuja/Riyadh markets?
2. Are any of your team already using AI tools for research or client communications, and is that happening in a consistent or ad-hoc way?
3. With sensitive family and minor data flowing between three countries, how are you currently thinking about what's safe to put into AI tools?
4. As a two-director firm, how do you decide which tasks stay founder-led versus what a trained consultant (or AI-assisted consultant) could own?
5. If your consultants could reclaim a day a week from admin, where would you want that time reinvested in the family experience?
