# Tangent — Discovery Brief
**Joe Adams, Co-Founder & Chief Product Officer** · Prepared 2026-06-30

## 1. Snapshot (≤3 lines)
- Tangent is an employee-referral platform that connects jobseekers from low socioeconomic backgrounds with tech-company employees for mentoring and referrals, to make hiring referrals less of a "privileged few" advantage.
- HR/recruiting tech (social-mobility / talent) · early-stage startup (founded 2022, ~$262K 2024 revenue, ~10 customers) · HQ London, UK.

## 2. Why now — the trigger
- Tangent closed a Seed round on 14 Apr 2025 (on top of its €1M/$1.1M April 2024 pre-seed; ~$2.56M raised over 4 rounds). Fresh capital plus a tiny team is exactly the window where founders standardize how the team uses AI before headcount scales — a natural reason to talk about AI upskilling and governance now rather than retrofitting later.
- As CPO of a product-led startup competing with incumbent referral/ATS tools, Adams is under pressure to ship faster with a lean team — a fit for AI adoption to automate repetitive product, ops, and go-to-market work.

## 3. Who you're talking to
- **Joe Adams — Co-Founder & CPO.** As product owner at a lean, newly funded startup, he likely cares most about getting more output per person (faster product/ops cycles) without adding headcount — directly served by AI upskilling for non-technical staff. LinkedIn: https://uk.linkedin.com/in/joe-adams-tangent
- **Gary Izunwa — Co-Founder & CEO.** Worth knowing only because at a ~5–15 person startup the buy decision will be founder-to-founder; the CEO will weigh budget and any AI-governance/diversity-data risk, so framing should address both productivity and responsible-AI use.

## 4. Value hypothesis
- A startup this size has no formal AI playbook; structured training for non-technical product, sales, and marketing staff turns ad-hoc ChatGPT use into repeatable workflows and establishes basic governance/security before they scale — the core of what we do.
- Proof point: we helped a client draw their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — relevant to a founder who must decide which roles to add vs. augment as the post-Seed team grows.

## 5. Discovery questions
1. Post-Seed, where do you most want product and go-to-market to move faster — and how much of that is repetitive work AI could absorb today?
2. How is your team using AI tools right now — is it standardized, or is everyone improvising on their own?
3. As you plan hiring against the new round, which roles are you debating augmenting with AI versus filling with headcount?
4. Tangent handles candidate and diversity data — do you have any guardrails today for how staff use AI with that data?
5. Of your non-technical functions — product ops, sales, marketing — which feels least confident with AI and would benefit first from training?

## 6. Opener
"Congrats on the Seed round in April — as you scale the team, I'd love to compare notes on getting your non-technical folks productive with AI before headcount ramps, so it's a standard, not a scramble."

## 7. Watch-outs (include ONLY if real)
- Very small, early-stage company (~$262K 2024 revenue, ~10 customers) — budget for external training may be tight; lead with ROI and role-saving, not a large program.
- Joe Adams is a common name; two LinkedIn profiles surfaced linking "Joe Adams" to "Tangent" (co-founder/CPO). The co-founder identity (LinkedIn/EE alumnus, co-founder with Gary Izunwa) is well corroborated, but confirm the exact title live before the call.
- No public evidence of any current AI-training vendor (BrainStation, General Assembly, AI Academy) in play — and no verified internal AI tooling. The AI-adoption pain in sections 2 and 4 is a hypothesis to confirm, not a confirmed fact.
