## 4. Value hypothesis
- Morgan Stanley's stated bottleneck is workforce enablement, not tooling — exactly the gap our AI training for non-technical teams (sales, marketing, ops) closes: adoption, automating repetitive tasks, governance, security, and best practices for ~80k employees. ([morganstanley.com](https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/ai-adoption-accelerates-survey-find))

## 5. Discovery questions
1. With ~50% firmwide access to AI tools, where is the gap today between employees who *have* access and those who can confidently use it in daily work?
2. How are you handling AI governance, security, and "best practice" guardrails for non-technical teams as adoption scales past pilots?
3. Following the changes in your Firmwide AI leadership, who now owns the employee-education and change-management mandate?
4. Outside wealth-management advisors (98%+ adoption), which functions — e.g., internal sales, marketing, operations — are furthest behind on AI fluency?
5. As roles shift with AI, how are you mapping which jobs to reskill, redeploy, or create, and what would faster clarity there be worth?
