# Anthropic — Discovery Brief
**Boris Cherny, Creator & Head of Claude Code** · Prepared 2026-06-30

> Candor note: Boris Cherny is a real, verifiable Anthropic product leader, and Anthropic is itself a frontier AI lab — so it is an atypical prospect for an external "AI training for non-technical teams" service (they build the underlying models). Treat this as a partnership/co-sell or internal-enablement conversation rather than a standard buyer. Facts below are sourced; contact-specific items are marked where unverifiable.

## 1. Snapshot (≤3 lines)
- Anthropic builds frontier AI models (Claude) and AI products including Claude Code, an agentic coding/work tool. [anthropic.com/news/series-h]
- AI / foundation models · ~5,189 employees (May 2026) · HQ San Francisco, CA. [sacra.com/c/anthropic]

## 2. Why now — the trigger
- Anthropic filed confidentially for IPO (~$965B valuation) after a $65B Series H in May 2026 — explosive headcount growth ("tripled since the start of the year" per Cherny) means rapid onboarding of new, including non-engineering, staff to AI workflows. [techcrunch.com 2026-05-28; fortune.com 2026-06-08]
- Cherny's public thesis is "what happens after coding is solved" — AI is moving from engineers to all professional work; Anthropic's own fastest-growing internal Claude Code adopters are now management, sales, and legal (non-technical) roles. [lennysnewsletter.com; anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise]

## 3. Who you're talking to
- Boris Cherny — Creator & Head of Claude Code. Likely cares because he is publicly championing AI adoption beyond engineers ("after coding is solved") and owns the tool non-technical teams are increasingly using. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/bcherny

## 4. Value hypothesis
- As Anthropic scales headcount and pushes Claude/Claude Code into non-technical functions (finance, sales, design, legal), structured AI training + governance/best-practices for non-technical teams could accelerate safe adoption — directly aligned with their internal adoption data. [anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise]
- Proof point: we helped a client map their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — relevant to a company restructuring work around AI agents and rethinking the "software engineer" title.

## 5. Discovery questions
1. As headcount triples, how are you onboarding non-engineering hires (sales, design, finance, legal) to AI workflows today — self-serve, or structured enablement?
2. Your data shows management, sales, and legal are the fastest-growing internal Claude Code adopters — where is that adoption hitting friction or governance gaps?
3. "After coding is solved," how are you thinking about reskilling non-technical teams versus reorganizing the roles themselves?
4. What does AI governance and best-practice enforcement look like for non-technical employees using agents day-to-day?
5. Where do you see the gap between what Claude Code can do and what non-technical staff actually know how to do with it?

## 6. Opener
"Boris — you've been vocal that the real story is what happens after coding is solved; with headcount tripling this year, I'd love to compare notes on how you're getting non-engineering teams productive and governed on AI that fast."

## 7. Watch-outs (include ONLY if real)
- Conflict of interest: Anthropic builds the models and Claude Code itself — they have deep in-house AI expertise and their own enablement content ("How Anthropic teams use Claude Code"). External AI training is a hard sell; position as partnership/co-sell to their enterprise customers rather than internal training. [anthropic.com PDF: How Anthropic teams use Claude Code]
- Tooling: heavy internal use of Claude Code and Claude in their productivity/finance suite — any offering must complement, not compete with, their own stack.
