# Keen Technologies — Discovery Brief
**John Carmack, Founder — Prepared 2026-06-30**

> Fit note (candid): This is a poor fit for our offering. John Carmack is a world-renowned programmer (Doom, Quake, ex-CTO of Oculus/Meta) running a frontier AGI *research* lab staffed by elite AI scientists (e.g., Richard Sutton). They are not a "non-technical team" needing entry-level AI adoption training. Treat as a brand/relationship play at best, not a real sales prospect. Contact-specific role confirmed; everything else qualified below.

## 1. Snapshot (≤3 lines)
- An AGI research startup pursuing foundational breakthroughs in computational intelligence (e.g., teaching robots to learn video games via reinforcement learning from real-world camera/joystick setups).
- Industry: AI/AGI research · Size: Unknown (small research team; only ~$20M raised) · HQ: Dallas, Texas.

## 2. Why now — the trigger
- In a Nov 2025 interview, Carmack revealed Keen recently built a system merging robotics, simulation, and reinforcement learning (camera-on-TV + robot-on-joystick to learn games) — a sign the lab is moving from pure research toward applied experiments. (Note: this signals research momentum, not a training-buying need.)
- No new funding since the $20M round (Aug 2022); the most "recent event" of substance is the Nov 2025 interview. Material commercial trigger for *our* offering: none found.

## 3. Who you're talking to
- **John Carmack — Founder.** Likely cares only about deep technical AGI problems, not workforce AI-adoption training; he is among the most technical people in the industry, so our value proposition does not map to him. LinkedIn URL: Unknown (could not verify an official, current profile).
- No other stakeholder identified that would change the approach.

## 4. Value hypothesis
- Honest assessment: our core offering (AI training for non-technical design/sales/marketing teams) has **no clear mapping** to a frontier AGI research lab. The only conceivable angle is AI governance/best-practices or org-design for any non-research support staff they hire — speculative and unverified.
- Most relevant proof point if forced to use one: we helped a client draw their AI org chart and hire/save roles 50% faster — potentially relevant only if Keen ever scales a non-research team (no evidence they are).

## 5. Discovery questions
1. As Keen moves from research toward applied robotics/RL experiments, are you building out any non-research functions (ops, comms, BD) that would need AI fluency?
2. How do you think about AI governance and best-practices internally — is that handled entirely by the research team, or is there appetite for structured frameworks?
3. With a lean Dallas team, where do repetitive or non-core tasks consume time you'd rather spend on research?
4. As you hire, how are you mapping which roles AI can augment versus replace across the org?
5. Beyond the core AGI work, are there any team-enablement gaps you've felt as the company grows post-2022?

## 6. Opener
"John — saw the Nov 2025 piece on Keen's camera-and-joystick RL system; the move toward applied robotics experiments is fascinating. I'll be upfront: we train non-technical teams on AI adoption, which likely isn't your core need — but I'd value 10 minutes to learn whether any non-research functions you're scaling could use it."

## 7. Watch-outs (include ONLY if real)
- Hard disqualifier: Carmack is a top-tier AI/engineering expert; pitching entry-level AI training risks credibility. Lead with humility or deprioritize.
- Tiny capital base ($20M, no new rounds since 2022) and a research-only profile mean limited budget/appetite for L&D services.
